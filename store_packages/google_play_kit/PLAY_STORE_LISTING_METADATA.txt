Spadas Lens turns your smartphone camera into a high-speed optical resale scanner. Simply point your camera down thrift store aisles, video game shelves, sneaker walls, or designer clothing racks to identify items, pull verified eBay sold comps, and calculate your exact net profit in real time.

• Tactile Haptic & Audio Feedback: High-pitch audio chimes and distinct vibrations trigger the moment a profitable item is detected.

💰 REAL-TIME SOLD COMPS & TRUE NET PROFIT
